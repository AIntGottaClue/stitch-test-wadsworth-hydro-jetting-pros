TECHNICAL INQUIRIES

MODERN SUBDIVISION HYDRO JETTING FAQ

Direct engineering answers regarding thermoplastic pipes, pipe sags, and high-velocity cleaning in Wadsworth.

help Can high-pressure hydro jetting crack or rupture SDR-35 PVC pipe?

Not when calibrated properly. While industrial jetting units operating above 4,000 PSI can damage lightweight plastics, our modern residential equipment is strictly regulated between 2,200 and 2,800 PSI. This delivers maximum hydraulic stripping capacity while staying well within the structural hoop strength and joint integrity limits of Schedule 40 ABS and SDR-35 PVC.

help How does hydro jetting resolve pipe bellies (sags) caused by Medina clay settling?

A pipe belly creates a low point where standing wastewater loses flow velocity, causing heavy solids, silts, and grease to drop out of suspension. While hydro jetting cannot physically lift a sagged pipe back to grade, our 12 to 16 GPM fluid sweep completely evacuates the accumulated sediment trap, returning the entire diameter of the line to free flow and preventing repetitive clogs.

help Why are newer homes in Silvercrest experiencing line backups so soon?

Contemporary subdivisions often suffer from construction-phase washouts—such as dry mortar dust, tile grout rinse, subsoil backfill wash-in, and heavy trench gravel entering during construction—combined with high concentrations of modern synthetic laundry detergents that emulsify and coat plastic walls.

help Will jetting displace the rubber mechanical gaskets between PVC sections?

No. Our crews use radial, low-angle propulsion nozzles with rounded guide noses designed specifically to bridge gasketed hub gaps safely. By contrast, sharp steel mechanical cable snakes commonly hook into rubber gaskets, pulling them into the line and causing severe structural failure.
