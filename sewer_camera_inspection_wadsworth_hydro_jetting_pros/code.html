PRE-JETTING VS. POST-JETTING OPTICAL PROTOCOL

High-pressure hydro jetting is an aggressive, industrial-strength scouring method. Camera verification is mandatory both before striking the line and following completion to ensure maximum safety and verified cleanliness.

search_check PHASE 01: PRE-SCOUR DIAGNOSTIC
PRE-JET

Before introducing 4,000 PSI hydro jetting pressure, our technicians conduct a full optical pass to map the structural profile and ensure the conduit can withstand hydraulic scouring.

  • check_circle Material Substrate Identification: Confirms whether the conduit is Cast Iron, Vitrified Clay, PVC, Orangeburg, or Asbestos Cement.
  • check_circle Structural Integrity Clearance: Verifies walls are not cracked or collapsed, which would cause high-pressure water to blowout surrounding soil.
  • check_circle Tooling & Nozzle Selection: Matches the blockage type (grease, roots, scale) to specific jet heads (penetrating, rotary chain, cornering nozzle).
  • check_circle Target PSI Calibration: Sets proper pump pressure limits based on the identified pipe material and age.
Operational Objective Establish zero-risk operating parameters and identify obstacle coordinates before water deployment.
verified PHASE 02: POST-SCOUR VERIFICATION
POST-JET

A second camera run immediately follows hydro jetting. Mechanical snakes only punch small holes; post-scour optical recording verifies that total circumference restoration has been achieved.

  • verified_user 360° Wall Scour Confirmation: Confirms elimination of fats, oils, and grease (FOG) along the upper crown and base flow line.
  • verified_user Flush Root Collar Shearing: Verifies that invasive tree roots have been cut flush to the pipe wall rather than merely frayed.
  • verified_user Clean Joint Assessment: Discloses underlying joints previously masked by sludge to catch shifts before backups recur.
  • verified_user Definitive Proof of Restored Capacity: Delivers visual confirmation that full flow volume has returned across the entire line.
