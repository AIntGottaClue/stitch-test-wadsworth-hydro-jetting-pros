Fluid Mechanics & Chemistry

FOG Emulsification Chemistry & Thermal Hydraulic Dynamics

Grease blockages are not simple clogs; they are rigid saponified structures formed when fatty acids react with alkaline minerals and calcium inside wastewater lines. Traditional snakes merely bore a pencil-thin aperture through the core, allowing grease to close back in within weeks.

Our rotary nozzle technology harnesses high-velocity 360-degree rotational spray patterns. The sheer velocity disrupts the crystalline fat matrices, breaking lipids into microscopic colloidal droplets. Combined with targeted water volumes, the suspended emulsion is carried completely beyond the building trap into high-flow city lines without redepositing.
