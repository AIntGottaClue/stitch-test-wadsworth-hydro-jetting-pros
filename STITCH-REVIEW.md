# Google Stitch review: Wadsworth Hydro Jetting Pros

Reviewed October 4, 2026 against the current **Single City Affiliate Lead Gen - The Complete 2026 SEO System Checklist**.

## Verdict

Stitch made a strong visual prototype, not a working lead-generation website. It understands the service, builds clear page sections and puts calls and forms in useful places. The exported pages do not form a connected site, the forms cannot deliver leads, and several display success messages without sending anything. The content also claims unverified operating capabilities and expands beyond the checklist's one-city scope.

This is an unchanged test, not a production release. No issues below were fixed. The only content change was replacing the zero-filled phone with **(877) 761-0283**, including `tel:+18777610283` and JSON-LD telephone values.

**Labels:** Right = supported by the export. Wrong = present but conflicts with the checklist. Missing = required evidence or implementation was not supplied. Missing research does not prove Stitch did no research; it means the delivered package does not document it.

## What was published

- Fourteen complete Wadsworth HTML page mockups, their original screenshots, the logo fragment and the supplied design document.
- The home mockup was copied to `index.html` so GitHub Pages has an entry point. Its original folder copy remains.
- `.nojekyll` tells Pages to serve the static files without Jekyll processing. No build step, CSS changes or base-path rewrite was needed.
- The unrelated saved Littleton Electric HTML in the ZIP was not published.
- All original `href="#"` links remain unchanged. The other mockups can be opened directly through the file links at the end of this review, but home-page navigation does not lead to them.
- No Cloudflare, analytics, Search Console, tracker or form integration was added. Those omissions are intentional for this test, not unauthorized work left unfinished.

## 0 · Business Brief

- **Right:** Site brand, niche, target city and residential/commercial customer types are clear.
- **Wrong:** "Direct Technician Routing", "our operators", branded rigs and dispatch language imply actual crews and equipment. No supplied provider record supports that identity or workflow.
- **Missing:** Approved affiliate destination, actual lead recipient, verified coverage, provider credentials, claim permissions and source register.
- **Wrong:** The hydraulic console labels a rig `WADSWORTH-UNIT-4` and shows operational status without evidence that such a unit exists. A simulator can be useful, but it is not proof of a real fleet.
- **Right:** No invented reviews or named staff biographies found. This does not validate the other operational claims.

## 2 · Keyword Map

- **Right:** Residential drain cleaning, commercial grease lines and camera inspection offer plausibly distinct customer needs.
- **Missing:** Research sources, dates, volume/difficulty data, organic-versus-map intent and approved primary cluster per page.
- **Wrong:** Home and the separate precision hydro-jetting page substantially overlap in purpose. Their distinct intent is not established.
- **Wrong:** Neighboring-city pages substitute a regional strategy for the one-city neighborhood strategy.

## 3 · Competitor Checks

- **Missing:** Dated competitor URLs, separate organic/map-pack observations, sitemap research, backlink review and conversion comparisons.
- **Right:** No competitor website is linked in the visible Wadsworth copy.
- **Missing:** Image provenance and proof that any borrowed reference informed layout only, rather than transferring another firm's identity or claims. The unrelated Littleton file is not Wadsworth evidence.

## 4a · Money Page

- **Right:** Clear service/city H1, hero phone CTA, short inquiry form, service explanations and repeated call actions.
- **Right:** Every complete page has exactly one H1. The logo fragment correctly is not a full landing page.
- **Right:** Process and obstruction comparisons help explain what hydro jetting does.
- **Wrong:** Very technical language makes the offer harder to scan: "hydraulic scouring", "subterranean analysis", "hydro-lock" and "optic telemetry" need plain-language review.
- **Wrong:** Guarantees and specifications are unsupported, including 1,500-4,000 PSI, 18 GPM and pipe-preservation promises. General technical plausibility is not proof of this provider's capability or a safe result on a particular pipe.
- **Wrong:** All fourteen page titles are the same `Wadsworth Hydro Jetting Pros`, not unique service/neighborhood titles.
- **Missing:** Seven of fourteen full pages lack a meta description; six lack a canonical tag.
- **Wrong:** Existing canonicals point to `wadsworthhydrojettingpros.com`, not the Pages test URL. They were preserved, not approved or verified as a production domain.
- **Wrong:** Form appearance is not lead delivery. The exact required `page_` fields and accepted-response behavior are absent.
- **Missing:** Licensed image provenance and honest illustrative labeling. Some informative images have no alt text.

## 4b · Content Capsule

- **Right:** Several location pages contain question headings followed by direct answers. Comparison diagrams and lists are useful formats.
- **Missing:** Inline evidence for the answers, named reviewer, review date and documented refresh triggers.
- **Wrong:** Answers confidently connect neighborhood geography, pipe materials, soil and drainage problems without supporting sources. These should not be treated as verified local diagnoses.
- **Right:** No cost-focused blog was exported. A blog is optional; its absence is not a failure.

## 4c · Page Map

- **Right:** The export includes a home, services hub, distinct services, process page, coverage hub and local mockups.
- **Wrong:** It is a collection of disconnected screens. Every non-phone anchor on the complete pages uses `#`, including service cards, header navigation and footer navigation.
- **Missing:** Working contact, privacy and terms pages. Footer labels exist, but their links are `#` and no corresponding documents were exported.
- **Wrong:** Export folder names and `code.html` files are not the checklist's `/services/` and `/neighborhood/` route structure.
- **Missing:** Approved URL/intent/owner/source map and an explicit indexing choice for this test.

## 5 · Launch

- **Right:** Static HTML works without an application build. Core page text is in HTML, not hidden in a JavaScript-only app.
- **Wrong:** Runtime Tailwind CDN processing and external Google fonts/image URLs are dependencies, not a self-contained optimized asset bundle.
- **Missing:** Compressed local WebP/AVIF assets, image licensing records and offscreen lazy loading. No `loading="lazy"` image attributes were found.
- **Missing:** Sitemap, robots.txt, favicon and complete Open Graph sharing metadata.
- **Wrong:** Repeated titles, missing descriptions, inconsistent canonicals and placeholder internal links fail production on-page/crawl checks.
- **Right, limited:** All fourteen full pages contain JSON-LD. Having JSON-LD is not the same as accurate schema.
- **Wrong:** Schema alternates `PlumbingService` and `Plumber`, repeats unsupported provider capabilities, and includes neighboring cities. Home `areaServed` includes Wadsworth, Doylestown, Rittman and Seville.
- **Missing:** Distinct accurate Service and BreadcrumbList markup, factual review and validator results. This review does not claim every existing schema type is invalid; accuracy and suitability remain unproved.
- **Missing:** Phone destination/receipt test, real form receipt, failure path and delivery-monitor owner. Phone markup was verified; no real call was placed.
- **Intentional test scope:** No Cloudflare, GA4 or Search Console. Do not score those as changes to make without permission.
- **Missing:** Lab performance measurements and field Core Web Vitals. Responsive appearance alone does not prove speed or CWV.

## 7 · Location Pages

- **Right:** Valley View/West Wadsworth, Historic Downtown/College Hill and Silvercrest pages attempt genuinely different local explanations rather than changing only a name.
- **Wrong:** Doylestown/Chippewa Township, Rittman and Seville are explicitly presented as additional towns/corridors. This is outside the single-city brief, regardless of whether a real provider might serve them.
- **Missing:** Verified neighborhood boundaries, coverage approval, housing/utility sources and current local permit evidence.
- **Wrong:** Local detail sounds authoritative but is unsourced. For example, Valley View is associated with cast-iron scale and the Rittman page asserts watershed-related silt and recurring basement backups.
- **Missing:** Working parent/neighborhood/service links, unique titles, proper neighborhood URLs, correct canonicals, sitemap entries and `page_` attribution.
- **Right:** Local questions and repeated call CTAs are included. No fake local review is needed or should be added to fill the missing proof.

## 8 · AI Visibility Routine

- **Missing:** Fixed question set, dated AI answers/citations, lead-quality baseline and an approved review owner.
- **Not a launch claim:** No observed AI citation, indexing result or ranking is claimed for this new test.
- **Intentional:** No monitoring was activated and no search/analytics accounts were touched.

## 9 · Link Tracker

- **Missing:** Eligible local/editorial opportunities, permission checks, prospect records and observed live links.
- **Right:** The export does not invent a GBP, membership, licence listing or outreach campaign. Nothing was submitted externally during this test.

## 10 · Outbound Links

- **Right:** No visible competitor, directory or price-source links were found.
- **Missing:** All actual factual claims need supporting local or niche sources with claim-specific inline links, checked dates and ownership/competitor checks.
- **Wrong:** A Wikipedia `sameAs` in Rittman JSON-LD is not a reader-visible source for that page's drainage and equipment claims.
- **Missing:** Link register and source verification. There is no end-of-page bibliography to remove; the problem is missing evidence, not bibliography formatting.

## 11 · Form and Tracker

This is the largest functional failure. Forms look finished but are prototypes.

- **Missing:** AirChatty external-tracking script and site-specific tracking ID on every page.
- **Missing:** `data-lead-form`, matching site form `id`/`name`, `page_site`, `page_location`, `page_code`, `page_details`, honeypot `website`, and the exact `rf-name` / `full_name`, `rf-phone` / `phone`, `rf-email` / `email`, `rf-message` / `page_details` mapping.
- **Wrong:** IDs and names vary page by page. Some controls have neither a name nor an ID. The appendix lists the actual fields.
- **Wrong:** Commercial form handler prevents default submission, displays `formSuccess` and resets the form without a request.
- **Wrong:** Services hub immediately alerts `Transmission confirmed. Dispatch unit notified for Wadsworth address.` without sending anything.
- **Wrong:** Several other forms simply unhide a feedback block. That is not accepted CRM receipt.
- **Wrong:** Home prevents default submission in its inline script and shows a dispatch alert, again without a delivery request.
- **Missing:** Required US phone validation/normalization, confirmed-response-only Request Received card, 12-second failure handling, retained inputs and actual CRM test evidence.
- **Missing:** Actual recipient/privacy disclosure and applicable consent. No test leads were submitted on GitHub Pages.

## 12 · AI Citation (AEO-GEO)

- **Right:** Plain HTML explanations, questions and comparison structures give readers material to understand and quote.
- **Wrong:** Unsourced precision is the opposite of citation-ready evidence. Numerical performance and local infrastructure statements need real sources, not confident phrasing.
- **Missing:** One documented question/purpose per piece, research/gap/source record, reviewed outline, supported capsules, inline claim links and publish package.
- **Wrong:** There are zero working internal content links; the recommended links cannot be satisfied by `#`.
- **Missing:** Repeated dated citation observations and refresh register. No AI tool can guarantee a citation.

## Lead Gen Recommendations and Template Change Log

- **Wrong:** Presenting mockup dispatch, equipment and guarantees as provider facts conflicts with the checklist's no-invented-facts rule.
- **Missing:** Traceable implementation decisions, provider/coverage evidence and production acceptance record.
- **Right:** This publication respects the requested limited test: no redesign, navigation wiring, lead integration or search rollout. The changes are recorded above rather than silently fixing the prototype.

## Priority if this ever becomes a real lead site

1. Confirm the actual provider, affiliate workflow and one-city coverage; remove unsupported guarantees and capabilities.
2. Build working page routes and truthful privacy/terms/contact pages.
3. Implement the exact form/tracker contract and verify receipt on the approved live domain, not this preview.
4. Research and cite local/technical claims. Keep only approved in-city neighborhood pages.
5. Finish unique metadata, factual schema, correct canonicals, sitemap, robots, local licensed/compressed assets and performance QA.

These are review findings, not changes authorized by this test request.
