NORTHEAST OHIO SOIL PROFILE

MEDINA COUNTY GEOLOGICAL & CLIMATE CHALLENGES

The sewer mechanics of Wadsworth and Medina County are governed by Northeast Ohio's unique glacial till. Unlike sandy or rocky substrates, local subsurface geology places mechanical strain directly onto subterranean pipes.

terrain Heavy Glacial Clay Soil

Medina County features dense Ellsworth and Mahoning silt loams with high clay fractions. Saturated clay swells immensely, gripping pipe joints and creating uneven shearing loads that open hairline separations.

ac_unit Freeze-Thaw Soil Movement

Harsh winter frost lines penetrate 36 to 42 inches into the soil. Cyclic freezing and thawing heaves laterals, displacing rubber couplings, breaking hub seals, and creating pipe offsets where solids snag.

tsunami Seasonal Cloudburst Surcharge

Intense spring storms overwhelm collection systems. High hydrostatic pressure surges up reverse sewer paths into un-scoured basements whenever laterals contain unaddressed buildup or root clusters.
