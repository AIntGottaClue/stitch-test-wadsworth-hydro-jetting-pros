Procedural Architecture

Engineered Four-Phase Protocol

Each pipeline intervention executes under exact thermodynamic and optical parameters. We eliminate structural blind spots through rigorous mechanical standards.

01 Phase Alpha // Diagnostic

Digital Optical Reconnaissance

We insert a high-definition self-leveling push-rod optical camera through the primary municipal or residential cleanout. The objective is systematic structural inspection: identifying pipe composition (Vitrified Clay Pipe / VCP, cast iron, or SDR-35 PVC), measuring internal wall delamination, cataloging root penetrations at pipe collars, and calculating pipe pitch or line bellies.

Metric Captured: Substrate & Root Depth Profile
02 Phase Beta // Calibration

Hydraulic Pressure & Nozzle Calibration

Selecting the exact mechanical nozzle profile to match blockage density and host pipe structural integrity. We configure pump operational pressures between 1,500 and 4,000 PSI with volumetric flow rates up to 18 GPM. Aging vitrified clay lateral joints are capped at non-destructive PSI levels, while hardened cast iron interior scale is remediated with high-torque chain-flail heads.

Dynamic Tolerance: 1,500 – 4,000 PSI @ 18 GPM
03 Phase Gamma // Execution

Controlled High-Velocity Scouring

The high-tensile hose enters the lateral. Rear-facing precision water orifices angled at 45 degrees provide massive forward thrust while simultaneously scrubbing the 360-degree pipe inner perimeter. Forward-penetrating penetrating jets bore into compacted organic obstructions, mineral scale, and heavy root clusters, washing all loosened debris downstream toward the municipal sewer main.

Shearing Action: 360° Circumferential Scour
04 Phase Delta // Verification

Optical Pass-Through & Flow Testing

Once scouring is concluded, the fiber-optic camera assembly is re-inserted for post-run forensic analysis. We visually confirm full-bore restoration of line diameter, total eradication of grease collars, and complete evacuation of root tendrils. Sustained high-volume water introduction confirms uninterrupted gravity flow through the city connection point.

Restoration Standard: 100% Full-Bore Clearance

Comparative Engineering

Hydrodynamic Jetting vs. Mechanical Snaking

Mechanical mechanical snaking punctures a temporary hole through an obstruction; hydrodynamic scouring remediates the full pipe diameter back to bare conduit walls.

Parameter Hydrodynamic Jetting Conventional Mechanical Cable (Snake)
Cross-Section Clean Radius 100% full-bore removal of grease, scale, mineral build-up, and root filaments. Leaves outer 70-80% of debris lining pipe wall; punches small puncture path.
Pipe Wall Abrasion Impact Zero mechanical contact. Water velocity acts uniformly without wall gouging. High friction; steel rotational blades scrape and score brittle clay and cast iron.
Debris Evacuation Direction Rear jets carry suspended particles out to municipal sewer mainline interceptor. Chewed solids settle downstream in bellies, frequently forming secondary dams.
Grease & FOG Emulsification Complete liquid emulsification under high-pressure shear. Slices through soft grease; does not remove grease film from pipe circumference.
Verification Standard Digital optical camera audit conducted prior to and immediately following run. Tactile feedback only; line condition remains completely blind.
